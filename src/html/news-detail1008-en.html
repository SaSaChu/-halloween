2025 LOHAS Taoyuan｜Love Enchanted Realm · Romantic Taoyuan

Are you ready to encounter your destined love?
This year, Taoyuan Halloween City introduces the romantic yet mysterious “Love Enchanted Realm · LOHAS Taoyuan,” where sparks of love begin to bloom throughout the city.

  • Event Date： 114/11/1 (Sat) 13:00-18:30
  • Event Venue： Taoyuan Arts Plaza · Fenghe Park · 77 Arts Town
  • Eligibility： Unmarried singles aged 25–45, who are registered residents of Taoyuan City or currently studying or working in Taoyuan.
  • Participant Limit： 30 couples (60 participants in total — 30 men and 30 women)
  • Registration Fee： I Love You” Singles Special – NT$520 per person

Event Highlights

  • Destiny Matching Wheel – Let fate spin and bring your match to you!
  • Fun Interactive Missions – Break the ice easily and make new connections through playful challenges.
  • Romantic Encounters at 77 Arts Town – Create unforgettable memories in a charming Japanese-style cultural setting.
Registration Period: From now until October 21, 2025, or until all spots are filled!
Special Gift: Participants who successfully register and attend the full event will receive an exclusive Taoyuan Halloween City souvenir.
