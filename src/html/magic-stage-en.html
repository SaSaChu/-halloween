2025 LOHAS Taoyuan-Haunted Castle Carnival: Golden Hits Party

Stage Broadcasts & Performance Information

"Haunted Castle Carnival: Golden Hits Party” is one of Taoyuan’s most iconic annual brand events — Taoyuan Halloween City. With “LOHAS Taoyuan” as its core spirit, the event blends local culture, tourism highlights, and the dynamic energy of Golden Melody performances.

Renowned Golden Melody Award–winning singers and popular bands will take turns performing on stage, igniting the crowd with excitement. From the vibrant daytime market to the high-energy nighttime concerts, visitors can experience the lively rhythm of Taoyuan from day to night.

This party is not just a music festival — it’s a grand stage that showcases Taoyuan’s cultural charm and tourism vitality.

Family Magical Fun City

Stage Broadcast & Show Info

In 2025, Taoyuan Halloween City will feature two specially planned family-friendly events, inviting the beloved YOYO Family hosts from Eastern Broadcasting to take the stage for a lively sing-and-dance party!

The YOYO brothers and sisters will appear in fun and creative costumes, leading children and parents to sing and dance together — creating joyful and unforgettable memories for all.
